How to review

  1. Pick a piece from the tabs at the top. Each line is one stretch of speech.
  2. Tap ▶ Play on a line and listen. Fix the words in the box so they match exactly what was said, including repeats and "umm" only if they matter. Do not summarise.
  3. Set the speaker: type or pick the name or role, e.g. Sindhu HL, HOS, RAS. "Unknown A/B/C" are guesses from the computer; the same letter is usually the same voice within a piece.
  4. When the line is right, tap ✓ Confirm. It turns green.
  5. Can't make it out? Write what you can, put [unclear] for the missing words, and add a short note.
  6. Orange lines ("several voices") are where people talk over each other: check carefully who said what.
  7. Your work is kept in this browser, but tap "Download my edits" and send that file to the coordinator when you stop, even if you are not finished. Send the newest file each time.

Laptop shortcuts: Ctrl+Space play the line you are in, Ctrl+Enter confirm and jump to the next line.

Switching device? Open your page on the new device and use Load an edits file to continue from your last downloaded file.
